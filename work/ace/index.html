Work / 04

ACE

A music player built around a reel of tape.

SectorMusic / interaction
RoleInteraction · Motion · App
Year2026

The brief

Music apps reduce a track to a progress bar. ACE gives it a body again: a tape reel that turns while the music plays, and that you turn to move through it.

What we did

  1. Made a tape reel the centre of the player, labelled like a tape box: title, date, master format.
  2. The reel turns with the music: one full turn every twenty seconds.
  3. Grab the reel and turn it to scrub, forwards or back.
  4. Kept everything else quiet: one play button, a countdown, a plain track list.

Where it is now

A private tool, used every day on one Mac.
