BeiCare

Natural skincare, freshly made in New Zealand — the shop people see, and the iPad app that runs the workshop behind it.

SectorSkincare / made in NZ
RoleWeb · App · Operations
Year2026
Visitbeicare.co.nz ↗

The brief

A small maker blends hydrosols, oils and balms by hand in small batches. The shop had to feel as fresh as the product. The workshop needed one calm screen that says what to make today.

What we did

  1. Shaped the shop around the product: botanical, warm, made in small batches.
  2. Designed an iPad instrument panel for the workshop: today's batches, stock and work, on one screen.
  3. Made every action direct — drag a batch, tap a line, done.
  4. Built it bilingual: English first, Chinese one tap away.

Where it is now

The shop is live at beicare.co.nz. The workshop app is in daily use on iPad.
