MUZI

A New Zealand home and garden brand, from the soil bag to the online shop.

SectorHome & garden / retail
RoleBrand · Photography · E-commerce
Year2026
Visitmuzi.co.nz ↗

The brief

A specialist range of succulent soils, stones and pots needed to look as considered as the plants they hold — on the shelf, in store and online.

What we did

  1. Shaped the identity and the voice: Home & Garden, quiet and earthy.
  2. Photographed the range in one consistent light.
  3. Organised a catalogue of around three hundred products.
  4. Built the shop at muzi.co.nz and the retail material around it.

Where it is now

The shop is live at muzi.co.nz, with stock in Auckland retail.
