Privacy

What this site collects, why, and who helps us run it. In plain words.

Last updated 5 November 2026

Who we are

Oeuvre is a trading name of BNB Management Consulting Ltd, based in Auckland, New Zealand. We are responsible for this website and for the information it collects.

Questions or requests: info@bnbmanagement.co.nz.

Visitor analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used: pages visited, time on page, scrolling and clicks, the type of device and browser, and an approximate location. Clarity also makes anonymous recordings of how a page is used, which we watch to find what is confusing or broken.

Clarity sets cookies to recognise a returning browser. Anything typed into the conversation box, and the conversation itself, is hidden from these recordings. Microsoft processes this data under its own privacy statement and keeps it for a limited time.

The figure on the home page

The figure does not use your camera. Its movement comes from video recorded in advance.

When you type or speak to it, your message is sent to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the reply, and the reply is sent to ElevenLabs, which turns it into speech. We keep the text of conversations on our server for up to 90 days, without your IP address and with email addresses and phone numbers masked, so we can see what visitors ask and improve the site. Each morning Anthropic's Claude model reads the previous day's conversations and writes a short summary that is emailed to us. Please do not share anything you would not want us to read. To prevent abuse we keep counts of messages and costs, and a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address for about an hour.

Voice input uses your browser's own speech recognition, and only while you hold the space bar or tap the microphone. Some browsers, such as Chrome, send that audio to their provider to turn it into text.

Other services

The site is hosted by Hostinger, whose servers keep standard access logs (such as IP address, time and page requested). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a font is fetched.

Your browser remembers a few settings for this site only, such as whether sound is on and whether you have seen the opening animation. These stay on your device.

Where your information goes

Microsoft, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Google and Hostinger may process information outside New Zealand, including in the United States. We use them because they apply security and privacy protections we consider comparable to New Zealand's.

Email

If you write to us, we use what you send only to reply and to work with you, and we keep it for as long as we need it for that purpose and our records.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Write to info@bnbmanagement.co.nz.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes

We will update this page when what we collect changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
