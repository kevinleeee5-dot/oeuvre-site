Privacy

What we collect, why we need it, and who is involved in handling it. Here it is, plainly.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who we are

Oeuvre is a trading name of BNB Management Consulting Ltd, based in Auckland, New Zealand.

We are responsible for this website and for the information collected through it.

If you have any questions, write to info@bnbmanagement.co.nz.

Visit statistics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used: the pages visited, time spent, scrolling and clicks, device and browser type, and the general region of the visit.

This helps us find designs that are not intuitive, features that are broken, and experiences that need improving.

Clarity uses cookies to recognise repeat visits and may record how a page is used. In those recordings, anything typed into the chat box and the conversation text is masked.

Microsoft processes this data under its own privacy policy and stores it under its own retention rules.

The figure on the home page

The figure on the home page is made of pre-recorded video. It does not use your camera.

If you choose to talk to it, the text you type, or the transcript of your speech, is sent to Anthropic, where Claude writes the reply. The reply is then passed to ElevenLabs to be turned into speech.

To understand what visitors need and keep improving the site, we keep the conversation text on our server for up to 90 days.

Saved conversations carry no IP address, and email addresses and phone numbers are masked automatically. We also use Claude to tidy the previous day's conversations into a short summary that is emailed to the site team.

Please do not share private or sensitive information in the chat that you would not want us to see.

To prevent abuse, we record message counts and service costs, and keep a hashed form of your IP address briefly, cleared after about an hour.

Speech recognition is provided by your browser and is only active while you hold the space bar or actively tap the microphone. Some browsers may send the recording to their provider to be transcribed.

Other services

The site is hosted by Hostinger. The server may keep standard access logs, including IP address, time of visit and the page requested.

The site's fonts are loaded through Google Fonts. In that process, Google may receive your IP address.

We also save a few site preferences on your device, such as the sound setting and whether you have seen the opening animation. These settings stay in your browser only.

Where information goes

Some of the providers we use, including Microsoft, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Google and Hostinger, may process data outside New Zealand, including in the United States.

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies. We choose third parties according to what the service needs and pay attention to how they protect data.

Email

If you contact us, we use the information you give to reply, to discuss working together or to handle your request.

These messages are kept for as long as is needed to do the work and to keep reasonable records, and are not used for unrelated purposes.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to ask for the personal information we hold about you and to ask for it to be corrected.

Please contact us at info@bnbmanagement.co.nz.

If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can also contact the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

Updates

When the way we collect or use information changes, we will update this page.

The date at the top shows the latest version.
