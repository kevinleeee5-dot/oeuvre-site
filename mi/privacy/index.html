Tūmataiti

Ngā mōhiohio e kohia ana e mātou, te take e hiahiatia ana, me ngā tāngata e whai wāhi ana ki te whakahaere. Anei, kua whakamāramahia.

Whakahoutanga whakamutunga: 5 Whiringa-ā-rangi 2026

Ko wai mātou

Ko Oeuvre te ingoa hokohoko o BNB Management Consulting Ltd, kei Tāmaki Makaurau, Aotearoa.

Ko mātou te kawenga mō tēnei paetukutuku me ngā mōhiohio e kohia ana mā roto i a ia.

Mehemea he pātai tāu, tuhia mai ki info@bnbmanagement.co.nz.

Ngā tatauranga haerenga

Ka whakamahi mātou i a Microsoft Clarity hei mōhio ki te whakamahinga o te paetukutuku: ngā whārangi i tirohia, te roa o te noho, te retireti me ngā pāwhiri, te momo pūrere me te pūtirotiro, me te rohe whānui o te haerenga.

Mā tēnei e kitea ai ngā hoahoa kāore i māmā te mārama, ngā āhuatanga kua pakaru, me ngā wheako hei whakapai ake.

Ka whakamahi a Clarity i ngā pihikete hei mōhio ki ngā hokinga mai, ā, ka taea te hopu i te āhua o te whakamahi i tētahi whārangi. I aua hopunga, ka huna ngā tuhinga i te pouaka kōrero me te kupu o te kōrerorero.

Ko Microsoft te kaiwhakahaere o ēnei raraunga i raro i tāna kaupapahere matatapu, ā, ka rongoā i raro i ōna ake ture pupuri.

Te kanohi o te whārangi tuatahi

He ataata kua oti kē te hanga te kanohi o te whārangi tuatahi. Kāore e whakamahi i tō kāmera.

Mehemea ka kōrero koe ki a ia, ka tukua ngā kupu e tuhia ana e koe, te tuhinga rānei o tāu kōrero, ki a Anthropic, ā, ka tuhia e Claude te whakautu. Ka tukua taua whakautu ki a ElevenLabs kia huri hei reo.

Kia mārama ai ki ngā hiahia o ngā manuhiri, kia pai ake ai te paetukutuku, ka pupuri mātou i ngā kupu kōrerorero ki tā mātou tūmau mō te 90 rā i te nuinga.

Kāore he wāhitau IP i ngā kōrerorero kua tiakina, ā, ka huna aunoa ngā wāhitau īmēra me ngā nama waea. Ka whakamahi hoki mātou i a Claude hei whakarāpopoto i ngā kōrerorero o te rā o mua, ā, ka tukua taua rāpoto ma te īmēra ki te kapa paetukutuku.

Kaua e tuku i ngā mōhiohio tūmataiti, hōhonu rānei e kore koe e hiahia kia kite mātou i te kōrerorero.

Hei ārai i te whakamahi hē, ka tuhi mātou i te tatau karere me te utu ratonga, ā, ka pupuri poto i tētahi āhua haehae o tō wāhitau IP, ka mukua i muri i te kotahi haora pea.

Nā tō pūtirotiro te mōhio ki te reo, ā, e kaha ana anake i te wā e pupuri ana koe i te pātuhi mokowā, e pāwhiri ana rānei koe i te hopuoro. Tērā pea ka tukua e ētahi pūtirotiro te hopunga ki tā rātou kaiwhakarato kia tuhia.

Ētahi atu ratonga

Ka manaakitia te paetukutuku e Hostinger. Tērā pea ka pupuri te tūmau i ngā rārangi uru māori, tae atu ki te wāhitau IP, te wā o te haerenga me te whārangi i tonoa.

Ka utaina ngā momotuhi o te paetukutuku mā Google Fonts. I roto i tērā tukanga, tērā pea ka whiwhi a Google i tō wāhitau IP.

Ka tiakina hoki e mātou ētahi manakohanga paetukutuku ki tō pūrere, pēnei i te whakaritenga oro me te kitenga o te hākoritanga whakatuwhera. Ka noho ēnei i tō pūtirotiro anake.

Te wāhi e haere ai ngā mōhiohio

Tērā pea ka tukatuka ētahi o ngā kaiwhakarato e whakamahia ana e mātou, tae atu ki a Microsoft, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Google me Hostinger, i ngā raraunga ki waho i Aotearoa, tae atu ki Amerika.

Ka whakahaere ēnei kaiwhakarato i ngā mōhiohio i raro i ō rātou ake kaupapahere matatapu. Ka kōwhiri mātou i ngā tuatoru e ai ki ngā hiahia o te ratonga, ā, ka aro ki tā rātou tiaki raraunga.

Īmēra

Mehemea ka whakapā mai koe, ka whakamahi mātou i ngā mōhiohio kua homai hei whakahoki kōrero, hei kōrero mō te mahi tahi, hei whakahaere rānei i tāu tono.

Ka pupuri ēnei karere mō te roa e hiahiatia ana hei whakahaere i te mahi me te pupuri rekoata e tika ana, ā, e kore e whakamahia mō ētahi atu kaupapa.

Ōu mōtika

I raro i te Ture Matatapu 2020 o Aotearoa, he mōtika tōu ki te tono kia kite i ngā mōhiohio whaiaro e pupuritia ana e mātou mōu, me te tono kia whakatikaina.

Whakapā mai ki info@bnbmanagement.co.nz.

Mehemea kāore koe i te harikoa ki tā mātou whakahaere i ō mōhiohio, ka taea hoki e koe te whakapā ki te Tari o te Kaihautū Matatapu o Aotearoa (privacy.org.nz).

Ngā whakahou

Ka whakahoutia tēnei whārangi ina huri te āhua o tā mātou kohi, whakamahi rānei i ngā mōhiohio.

Ko te rā i te upoko o te whārangi te putanga hou rawa.
