Te tīmatanga

Ko te nuinga ka tae mai me te rerenga kōrero kotahi: Kei te hiahia au ki tōku ake waitohu tiaki kiri. Ka āwhina mātou i a koe ki te whakatau he aha hei hanga, mā wai, me pēhea te āhua, ā, me pēhea te hanga me te hoko.

E ono ngā wāhanga

01

Te whakamahere hua

Ā mātou mahi
Mā wai, te utu, ngā momo hua me te raupapa whakarewa.
Ka whiwhi koe
He mahere hua: ngā hua, ngā rahi me te raupapa.
02

Te tātai me ngā tauira

Ā mātou mahi
Te rongo ki te kiri, te kakara, te kakano, ngā kaiwhakahohe me te utu e whāia ana — kātahi ka puta ngā tauira.
Ka whiwhi koe
Ngā tauira kua whakaaetia me tētahi whakatakotoranga hua.
03

Te hoahoa waitohu

Ā mātou mahi
Te ingoa, te kōrero, te moko, te tae me te momotuhi.
Ka whiwhi koe
He tuakiri waitohu me ngā kōnae hoahoa.
04

Te takai

Ā mātou mahi
Ngā pounamu, ngā tapanga me ngā pouaka — ka kimihia, ka whakahaerehia te tānga.
Ka whiwhi koe
Ngā kōnae kua rite mō te tā, ngā tauira takai me tētahi utu tūtohu.
Finish
Matte, blind emboss
Inks
Warm Gray 1C · 11C
05

Te paetukutuku me te ihirangi

Ā mātou mahi
Te kōrero o te waitohu, ngā whārangi hua me tētahi toa.
Ka whiwhi koe
He paetukutuku e mahi ana, ka tukuna atu ki a koe.
06

Te whakaputa me te tuku

Ā mātou mahi
Te kimi, te whakakī, te tapanga me te tākai.
Ka whiwhi koe
He hua kua oti, me ngā pūkete puranga.
